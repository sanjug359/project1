What Is Rainwater Harvesting?

Rainwater harvesting is the process of collecting rainwater from rooftops and storing it for later use. The water can be used for gardening, cleaning and other suitable purposes. Drinking water requires appropriate treatment and testing.

Benefits of Rainwater Harvesting

  • Reduces dependence on freshwater supplies.
  • Helps conserve water for dry periods.
  • Can reduce roof runoff during rainfall.
  • Encourages environmental awareness.
  • Can help reduce water expenses.

How Does It Work?

  1. Rain falls on the roof.
  2. Gutters collect and direct the water.
  3. Filters remove leaves and debris.
  4. A storage tank collects the water.
  5. The stored water is used for suitable purposes.

Formula Used

Rainwater (litres) = Roof Area (m²) × Rainfall (mm) × Collection Efficiency

One millimetre of rain falling on one square metre produces approximately one litre of water before losses.
